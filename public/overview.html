Section 01

User Roles & Permissions

TrueGear has 7 user roles. Each role has its own login and can only access what is relevant to their job. Below is a guide to every role in the system.

👑

Super Admin

Full access to everything — all modules, all users, all data. Manages the entire system and permissions.

🔍

QC Inspector

Performs vehicle inspections on arrival. Marks each checklist item as Pass, Fail, or NA and submits the report.

🛡️

Security Gate Keeper

Manages vehicle check-in at the workshop gate. Registers arriving vehicles, records odometer and takes entry photos.

🧑‍💼

Service Advisor

Front-line staff. Creates job cards, builds estimates, shares them with customers and tracks job progress end to end.

📦

Parts Manager

Manages the parts inventory. Confirms part availability for job cards and dispatches parts to the workshop bay.

🧑‍💻

Receptionist

Handles front desk operations — manages appointments, registers customers and vehicles, and supports walk-in entries.

👤

Customer

Receives the service estimate via WhatsApp or Email. Approves or rejects from their phone — no app required.

Role Permissions — Full Reference Table

Role Can See Can Do Cannot Do
👑 Super Admin All modules — Gate Entry, QC Inspection, Job Cards, Parts, Users, Dashboard, Appointments, Vehicle 360 Create / edit / delete users and roles, manage all permissions, access every module and report Nothing — full access
🔍 QC Inspector QC Inspection module only View, create, update and delete inspection reports for vehicles Cannot access Gate Entry, Job Cards, Parts, or User Management
🛡️ Security Gate Keeper Gate Entry module, vehicle records, customer details Check vehicles in, register new vehicles, record odometer and photos, create and update customer records Cannot create job cards, cannot access QC or Parts modules
🧑‍💼 Service Advisor Job Card module, service advisor dashboard, inspection reports Create job cards, build and share estimates, confirm jobs, track service progress, view and manage job card items Cannot manage users, cannot access Parts Manager module directly, cannot do Gate Entry
📦 Parts Manager Parts Manager module, parts inventory, parts requests from job cards View parts requests, confirm availability, dispatch parts to bay, add and update parts in inventory Cannot create job cards, cannot approve customer estimates, cannot access Gate Entry or QC
🧑‍💻 Receptionist Appointments, customer records, vehicle records, service advisor dashboard Book and manage appointments, register customers and vehicles, create and update service advisor records Cannot manage users or roles, cannot access Parts Manager or QC Inspection modules
👤 Customer Their own vehicle's estimate, line items, and total cost Approve or reject estimate via WhatsApp / Email link — no login required Cannot log into the workshop system. Cannot see other vehicles or any workshop data.
💡
How Roles Connect

The Receptionist books the appointment → the Gate Keeper checks the vehicle in → the QC Inspector inspects it → the Service Advisor builds the estimate → the Customer approves it → the Parts Manager dispatches parts → work begins. Every person only sees what they need.

Complete Workflow — Step by Step

This section walks through every step of the vehicle's journey in the workshop. Each workflow is explained simply with a visual flow and numbered steps.

2.1 — Appointment Booking (Receptionist)

The journey begins when a customer contacts the workshop to book a service. The Receptionist handles this from the front desk — capturing all details and reserving a time slot before the vehicle even arrives.

  • 1
    Customer Contacts Workshop

    Customer calls, walks in, or requests a booking. Receptionist opens the Appointment Booking screen from the dashboard.

  • 2
    Enter Customer Details

    Receptionist enters the customer's full name, mobile number and email. If the customer already exists in the system, their profile loads automatically — no re-entry needed.

    → Example: Mobile number 050-1234567 already registered → customer name and vehicle history loads instantly.

  • 3
    Enter Vehicle Details

    Receptionist enters the vehicle plate number. System performs a VIN lookup — if found, vehicle details (Make, Model, Year, Variant) are auto-filled. If not found, Receptionist enters manually.

    → Example: Enter "FAW J6 500" → System fills Make: FAW, Model: J6 500, Year: 2022 automatically.

  • 4
    Select Service Type & Requirements

    Receptionist selects what service the customer needs — B, C or D Service and whether it is AMC, Paid, Scheduled or Warranty. Any additional customer requests or notes are also recorded here.

  • 5
    Select Date & Time Slot

    Calendar shows available dates. On selecting a date, available time slots appear. Already-booked slots are greyed out and cannot be selected.

    → Validation: Cannot book a past date. Cannot book a slot that is already full.

  • 6
    Confirm Appointment

    Receptionist clicks "Confirm Appointment." System saves the record, generates a unique Appointment Reference Number, and sends a confirmation message to the customer's mobile.

    → Appointment Status changes to: SCHEDULED

What Happens on Appointment Day

When the customer arrives, the Gate Keeper searches by plate number or appointment reference. The system finds the booking and loads all pre-filled details — no re-entry needed. This saves time and avoids mistakes at the gate.

2.3 — QC Inspection by Inspector

After the vehicle is checked in, a QC Inspector performs a full physical inspection of the vehicle before any work begins. This protects the workshop and the customer by recording the vehicle's condition on arrival.

  • 1
    Open Inspection Form

    QC Inspector finds the vehicle on the dashboard and opens the inspection form. Vehicle and customer details are already pre-filled.

    → Vehicle status automatically changes to "Inspection (Draft)" the moment the form is opened.

  • 2
    Exterior Inspection (8 items)

    Inspector walks around the vehicle and marks each item as Pass ✅, Fail ❌ or NA — Body condition, Tyres, Headlights, Wipers, Side mirrors, Windshield, Indicators, Bumpers.

    → Example: Wiper blades marked as NA (item not applicable for this vehicle type)

  • 3
    Interior Inspection (8 items)

    Checks inside the vehicle — Dashboard warning lights, Air conditioning, Seat condition, Seatbelts, Horn, Steering play, Interior cleanliness.

  • 4
    Brake Test (3 items)

    Inspector or technician performs a brake test and records Brake Performance, Brake Noise, and Brake Vibration results.

    → Example: Brake fluid marked ❌ Fail with comment "level critically low"

  • 5
    Record Customer Complaints

    Inspector asks the customer: "Is there anything specific you want us to look at?" Complaints are typed into the system in plain text.

    → Example: "Engine makes a rattling noise when starting in cold weather"

  • 6
    Upload Vehicle Photos (Optional)

    Photos of damage, scratches, dents, or the odometer reading are uploaded directly from a phone or tablet. Stored securely in cloud — never lost.

  • 7
    Submit Inspection Report

    Inspector reviews the full checklist and clicks "Submit." The report is permanently saved. All Failed items automatically appear as Suggested Jobs on the Service Advisor's screen — ready to be added to the job card. Items marked NA are skipped.

    → Vehicle status changes to: INSPECTION DONE

💡
What Are Suggested Jobs?

After inspection, any item marked as FAIL is automatically shown to the Service Advisor as a recommended job. For example, if brake fluid fails, the SA sees "Replace Brake Fluid" as a suggestion when building the estimate. Items marked NA are not applicable and are ignored. The SA can accept or skip any suggestion.

Inspection ResultWhat It MeansWhat Happens Next
✅ PassItem is in good conditionNo action needed
❌ FailItem has a problem that needs immediate attentionAppears as Suggested Job for SA
— NAItem is not applicable for this vehicle type or checkSkipped — no action taken

2.4 — Job Card Creation

A Job Card is the official work order for every vehicle. It lists all jobs, parts, costs, and approvals in one place.

🧑‍💼 Service Advisor Opens Vehicle
↓
📋 Reviews Inspection ReportSuggested jobs appear automatically
↓
🔧 Selects Service Type + Category
↓
⚡ System Loads Parts List Automatically
↓
💰 SA Reviews + Adds Labour Charges
↓
💾 Save as Draft → Share with Customer
  • 1
    Open Vehicle Record

    Service Advisor clicks on the vehicle from their dashboard. The inspection report is already there — failed items appear as suggested jobs.

  • 2
    Select Service Type & Category

    SA picks the type of service (AMC, Paid, Warranty, Scheduled) and category (B, C, or D Service). This is the most important step — it tells the system what to do next.

  • 3
    Auto Parts Load

    Based on the service selection and vehicle model, the system automatically fills in the required parts with quantities and prices. No manual searching.

  • 4
    Add / Edit Items

    SA can add extra jobs, remove items, search parts from the master catalogue, and adjust quantities.

  • 5
    Apply Tax & Calculate Total

    SA selects the tax type (e.g., VAT 5%). System calculates Subtotal + Tax = Total Estimate automatically.

  • 6
    Save Job Card

    Job Card is saved as a DRAFT. A unique Job Card Number is generated. Status becomes JOB CARD DRAFT.

2.5 — Service Type Selection

The Service Type tells the system who is paying and what kind of agreement covers this service.

Service Type What It Means Who Pays? Example
AMC Service Annual Maintenance Contract — vehicle is on a fixed yearly maintenance plan Company / Corporate client pays a fixed annual fee A logistics company has 20 trucks on AMC. Monthly services are pre-agreed and auto-billed.
Paid Service Customer pays fully for all parts and labour on this visit Customer pays at the time of billing Mr. Ahmed brings his personal car for oil change and filter. He pays the full amount.
Scheduled Service A manufacturer-recommended periodic service (e.g., every 10,000 KM) Customer pays Vehicle has reached 50,000 KM. The owner brings it for the scheduled full service.
Warranty Service Vehicle or part is under warranty — manufacturer covers the cost Manufacturer / Dealer reimburses A gearbox failure occurs within warranty period. Parts replaced at no cost to customer.

⚠️ Partial Approval Flow

  • 1
    Customer Rejects Some Items

    Customer approves certain jobs but rejects others (e.g., approves oil change, rejects brake pad replacement). Partial approval submitted.

    → Example: Total AED 1,850 → Customer approves AED 750, rejects AED 1,100

  • 2
    SA Notified on Dashboard

    SA receives a notification. Dashboard shows which items were rejected. SA reviews the rejection and contacts the customer if needed.

  • 3
    SA Revises Job Card

    SA removes the rejected line items from the job card. Total recalculates automatically. SA clicks "Re-share Estimate."

  • 4
    Customer Approves Revised Estimate

    Customer receives the updated estimate and approves. Job card is now locked at the revised amount. Work begins on only the approved items.

    → Rejected items are noted in job history for future reference

2.10 — Job History Tracking

📂
Every job is saved permanently

Every vehicle visit creates a permanent history record — date, service type, parts used, technician, cost and photos. The next time the same vehicle arrives, the Service Advisor can see the full history instantly. This helps plan future services and identify recurring issues.

Section 03

Real Example — Complete End to End

Example Scenario 01

Mr. Ramesh — FAW J6 500 Truck — D Service (AMC)

Ramesh owns a fleet of trucks on Annual Maintenance Contract. One truck comes in for a Full D Service.

1
2.1 — Appointment Booking (Receptionist)

Sunday afternoon — Ramesh calls the workshop. Receptionist Kezia opens the Appointment Booking screen. Enters customer details (existing profile loads automatically), vehicle plate FAW-TRK-4821 (auto-fills as FAW J6 500, 2022), selects D Service + AMC, and books Monday 9:00 AM slot. Appointment confirmation sent to Ramesh via WhatsApp.

2
2.2 — Vehicle Walk-In / Entry

Monday 8:50 AM — Truck arrives at the gate. Gate Keeper types plate number FAW-TRK-4821 → system finds the appointment and auto-fills all customer and vehicle details. Entry photos taken (front, back, both sides). Odometer: 87,450 KM recorded. Vehicle status: VEHICLE IN.

3
2.3 — QC Inspection

9:00 AM — QC Inspector opens the inspection form. Performs 24-point check. 2 items FAIL: brake fluid critically low ❌, air filter clogged ❌. Several items marked NA (not applicable for this truck model). Customer complaint recorded: "Engine knock on cold startup." Inspection submitted. Status: INSPECTION DONE. Both failed items automatically appear as Suggested Jobs on Maria's screen.

4
2.4 — Job Card Creation

9:20 AM — Service Advisor Maria opens the vehicle on her dashboard. Reviews inspection report. Adds the 2 suggested failed items to the job card. Adds labour charges. Subtotal: AED 9,200. VAT 5%: AED 460. Total: AED 9,660. Saves as draft and shares estimate with Ramesh. Ramesh approves via WhatsApp link at 9:42 AM. Status: JOB CARD DRAFT → APPROVED.

5
2.5 — Service Type Selection

Maria selects Service Type: AMC Service. The job is linked to Ramesh's company Annual Maintenance Contract. Billing goes to the company account — no direct payment required from Ramesh at the time of service.

6
2.6 — Service Category (D Service)

Maria selects Service Category: D Service — Full Major Service. The system now knows the combination: FAW J6 500 + AMC + D Service and looks up the pre-configured parts master.

7
2.7 — Parts Auto-Loading

System auto-loads 12 parts in 2 seconds: Fuel Filter, Retarder Oil Filter, Grease Chassis, AC Dust Filter, Brake Fluid, Diff Oil, Oil Filter Bypass, Transmission Fluid and 4 more — all with part codes, quantities and prices pre-filled. Maria reviews and confirms. No manual searching required.

8
2.8 — Parts Consumption from Store

9:45 AM — Maria clicks "Request Parts." Store Manager receives all 12 parts on the dashboard. Confirms 11 available and dispatches to Bay 3. 1 part (Retarder Oil Filter) marked unavailable — expected same-day delivery. Maria informs Ramesh of the minor delay. Parts dispatched. Work begins at 10:00 AM. Technician marks each part as used — stock reduces automatically in real time.

9
2.9 — Send Estimated Job Card (Email / WhatsApp)

9:35 AM — Maria clicks "Share Estimate." Ramesh receives a WhatsApp message: "Your estimate of AED 9,660 is ready. Tap to approve." He also receives a full itemised PDF by email. At 9:42 AM Ramesh taps "Approve All" from his phone. Maria's dashboard instantly shows: APPROVED. Job card is locked — work proceeds on exactly what was agreed.

10
2.10 — Job History Tracking

4:00 PM — Work complete. Vehicle status: COMPLETED. Full service record saved permanently: Monday 14-Apr-2026, FAW J6 500, D Service AMC, 12 parts used, AED 9,660, Odometer 87,450 KM. Next time this truck arrives, the entire history is visible to the SA in one click.

Mr. Ahmed arrives without an appointment for a C Service. He finds the estimate too high and negotiates.

2.3 — QC Inspection

5

6

7
2.7 — Parts Auto-Loading

8
2.8 — Parts Consumption from Store

9

Maria removes the brake pad item, revises total to AED 750, and re-sends. Ahmed approves the revised estimate immediately. Job card locked at AED 750. Status: APPROVED.

10
2.10 — Job History Tracking
